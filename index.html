Barn CatMidnight Prowler

Mice want the grain. You're the barn cat. Keep them off it until the sun comes up.

PounceTap for a quick hop. Hold to wiggle: the pounce grows bigger, but mice nearby may spot you and bolt. Slide to aim, let go to leap.
CombosCatch several in one leap, or keep landing catches in a row.
Watch forRats eat faster. Bats swoop by for big points.

Get the app

Install Barn Cat on your phone. It opens full-screen and works without internet.

In Chrome, tap the menu ⋮ (top right), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Or download the Android app file (BarnCat.apk)

After it downloads, open it and tap Install. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser first.

Put Barn Cat on your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
