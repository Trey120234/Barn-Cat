# Barn Cat: Midnight Prowler

A small phone game: you're a barn cat protecting the grain from mice, rats and bats until dawn. Tap to pounce, hold to wiggle.

**Play:** https://trey120234.github.io/Barn-Cat/

**Get the app:**
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome and tap **Install Barn Cat** on the start screen, or download the app file from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trey120234/Barn-Cat/releases/latest).
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

All art and sounds are made in code. Fonts: Lilita One and Atkinson Hyperlegible, used under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
